GMC Misrepresentation Fix — Light Keepers Bookstore

Merchant Center ID: 5835431664

● 100% Resolved & Approved
01 / The Problem
Misrepresentation Policy Suspension

The Merchant Center account was entirely blocked under Google's Misrepresentation flag, disallowing all free product listings and stopping shopping ads from running.

02 / The Strategy
Comprehensive Policy & Merchant Audit

Updated physical address transparency, aligned refund/return clauses, cleaned shipping cost tables, and structured product GTIN/MPN attributes to meet strict Google merchant guidelines.

03 / The Result
Issue Cleared & Account Fully Restored

Received official Google clearance: "The requested review of Misrepresentation has been completed and the issue no longer appears in your Merchant Center account."
